# LIT Buy — Pre-handoff readiness checklist

This is the **operational sequence** between feature freeze and delivery to Claude Code/Workana. It does not replace [`ALPHA_SCOPE_AND_COMPLETION_CHECKLIST.md`](./ALPHA_SCOPE_AND_COMPLETION_CHECKLIST.md), which remains the highest authority for functional scope, completion, and production blockers.

States are intentionally limited to `CONCLUÍDO`, `EM ANDAMENTO`, and `PENDENTE`. “Concluído” here records evidence for a handoff step; it never means production-ready.

## Operating rule

Advance in the table order. Do not silently convert production blockers into Alpha work, treat CI as security approval, or execute future recommendations during repository-clarity work. Update a state only when the evidence named in its row exists.
