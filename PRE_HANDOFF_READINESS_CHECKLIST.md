# LIT Buy — Pre-handoff readiness checklist

This is the **operational sequence** between feature freeze and delivery to Claude Code/Workana. It does not replace [`ALPHA_SCOPE_AND_COMPLETION_CHECKLIST.md`](./ALPHA_SCOPE_AND_COMPLETION_CHECKLIST.md), which remains the highest authority for functional scope, completion, and production blockers.

States are intentionally limited to `CONCLUÍDO`, `EM ANDAMENTO`, and `PENDENTE`. “Concluído” here records evidence for a handoff step; it never means production-ready.

Manual browser findings, UX observations and future product decisions discovered during post-freeze stabilization are tracked in [`POST_FREEZE_BROWSER_QA_FINDINGS.md`](./POST_FREEZE_BROWSER_QA_FINDINGS.md). Entries there do not become Alpha blockers or authorized implementation merely by being recorded.

The final cross-profile functional acceptance before `PRODUCTION_HANDOFF.md` is governed by [`FINAL_FUNCTIONAL_VALIDATION_CHECKLIST.md`](./FINAL_FUNCTIONAL_VALIDATION_CHECKLIST.md). That checklist requires every visible operational capability to end explicitly classified and requires real capabilities to be exercised end-to-end where applicable; it still does not authorize Phase B, production or real money.

## Operating rule

Advance in the table order. Do not silently convert production blockers into Alpha work, treat CI as security approval, or execute future recommendations during repository-clarity work. Update a state only when the evidence named in its row exists. Browser/UX observations must be persisted in `POST_FREEZE_BROWSER_QA_FINDINGS.md`, classified before implementation, and must not silently broaden the feature-frozen Alpha. Final cross-profile acceptance must follow `FINAL_FUNCTIONAL_VALIDATION_CHECKLIST.md`; a capability may not be called complete merely because its page renders.
